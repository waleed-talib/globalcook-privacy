How We Use Your Information

AI-Powered Features

Our app uses Google Gemini API to provide AI cooking assistance. When you interact with the AI chef, your messages are sent to Google's servers for processing. We do not store your AI conversations permanently. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information about how Google handles this data.

Your data is stored on secure servers. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.
